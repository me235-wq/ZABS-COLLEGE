About Our College

Education with Purpose.
A Future with Possibilities.

Govt. ZABS College is an educational institution located in Pahrianwali, District Mandi Bahauddin, Punjab, Pakistan. The name ZABS stands for Zulfiqar Ali Bhutto.

The college is committed to providing students with opportunities for academic learning, personal development, and preparation for further education and future endeavours.

Dr. Muddasar Warraich

Principal
